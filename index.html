Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why "Cleaning Cheats"?

Because the best cleaning cheat is having someone else do it, and do it well. The name is about saving you time and effort. When it comes to the actual cleaning, we don't cut corners. We clean them.

How much does a cleaning cost?

Every space is different, so pricing depends on size, condition and how often you'd like service. Fill out our free quote form (it takes about two minutes) or call or text us, and we'll give you a clear price with no surprises.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Idaho Falls, Rigby, Rexburg and Island Park. If you're in a nearby community, just ask. We may still be able to help. See our service areas.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Many clients let us in with a door code, lockbox or key. We'll work out access details together when we set up your plan.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. We arrive with our own supplies and equipment. If you prefer specific products, or have surfaces that need special care, just let us know.

Can you handle Airbnb and vacation rental turnovers?

Yes. We schedule turnovers around your check-out and check-in times and let you know if anything needs attention before your next guests arrive.

What if something isn't quite right?

Just tell us. We want every clean to feel like a win, and we'll work with you to make it right.
